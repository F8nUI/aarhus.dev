aarhus.dev puts the city's developer meetups, talk nights, workshops and conferences in one timetable, so you can see what's on this week without following twenty groups on five different platforms.

It's kept up by the people who run the events and the people who go to them. Anyone can add an event or correct one, and the whole site is open on [GitHub](https://github.com/f8nui/aarhus.dev).

## Follow along

- **In your calendar:** [subscribe](webcal://aarhus.dev/calendar.ics), and every event shows up in your calendar app and stays up to date. Apps that ask for an address take `https://aarhus.dev/calendar.ics`.
- **In a feed reader:** [the RSS feed](/rss.xml) has every event still to come.
- **One at a time:** every event's page has links to add just that one to your calendar.

## Add your event

Running a meetup, a talk night or a conference in or around Aarhus that anyone can come to? [Add it](/add-event): fill in a form on GitHub, or open a pull request. It's free, and it's on the site a few minutes after it's accepted.
