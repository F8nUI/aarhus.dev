V2 Security is a conference and trade fair on cyber and information security, held each year in Aarhus and in Copenhagen by Teknologiens Mediehus, the media house behind Version2 and Ingeniøren. The Aarhus edition gathers over 1,500 attendees and about 50 security companies. Participation is free with registration.
