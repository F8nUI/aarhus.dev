Professor Christian Damsgaard Jensen from Aarhus University presents the aspects of digital sovereignty organisations must consider when moving away from Big Tech providers towards EU-based technology, infrastructure and services.

Rasmus Seeberg, VP of Engineering at iPaper, shares the motivations, considerations and experiences from migrating selected services from AWS to European providers.

The morning also includes a short introduction to TechCircle by Jesper Askov Bonde Petersen from Aarhus University's Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. Hosted by Tech Hub Aarhus in collaboration with TechCircle. Coffee and a light breakfast are served.
