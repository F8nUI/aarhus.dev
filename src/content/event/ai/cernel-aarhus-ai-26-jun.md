**Cernel** is a Danish AI startup that helps companies use AI and data to create real business value. In just a few years, they have grown from a young data science team into a company valued at more than DKK 100 million.

In this session, **CEO Andreas Busch** will openly share the journey behind Cernel.

From the early mistakes, pivots, and difficult decisions — to how a team that dropped out of university rebuilt the company from the ground up and created momentum in the AI market.

He will also share his key learnings on:

* AI strategy
* building AI companies
* product and go-to-market
* what actually works — and what does not work — right now

Afterwards, **Jacob, Head of AI** at Cernel, will dive into the more technical side of AI.

Among other things, he will demonstrate:

* how Cernel uses AI internally across the business
* how their AI stack and workflows operate in practice
* how they work with AI across the organization
* and how they see the future of AI evolving over the coming years

The session will combine founder honesty, concrete AI use cases, and technical perspectives from one of Denmark’s most exciting AI companies.
