An afternoon with Cernel, a Danish AI startup helping companies turn AI and data into real business value.

CEO Andreas Busch shares the story behind the company — the early mistakes, pivots, and decisions behind building an AI company, its product, and go-to-market. Jacob, Head of AI, then digs into the technical side: how Cernel runs its AI stack and workflows internally.

Founder honesty, concrete use cases, and technical perspectives from one of Denmark's most talked-about AI companies. Beverages provided.
