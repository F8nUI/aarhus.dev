An evening of talks, food, and networking at EIVA in Skanderborg, with a light dinner sponsored by EIVA and a company tour.

## Talks

**Self-Healing Rust: Fast Feedback Loops for Coding Agents** — Jordan Munch O'Hare shows the self-healing loop he uses in Rust projects: fast checks first, then targeted Clippy and tests based on what changed, with Cargo's JSON output helping the agent fix failures on its own.

**Bevy under the hood** — Ben Maguire explores how the Bevy game engine implements its Entity Component System, and what compromises it made along the way.
