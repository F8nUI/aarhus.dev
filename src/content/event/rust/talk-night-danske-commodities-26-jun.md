An evening of talks, food, and networking, with a light dinner sponsored by Danske Commodities.

## Talks

**Halloy — A native app for a dead protocol** — Casper Storm on building Halloy, a modern IRC client written in Rust, and why open protocols still matter.

**Nighttime Adventures** — Mikkel Wienberg Madsen explores three experimental control-flow features available on nightly Rust and their path to stabilization.

- Light dinner (vegan and non-vegan options)
