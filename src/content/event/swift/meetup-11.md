Our next meetup of Swift Developers, Aarhus will be on Tuesday April 21st, 2026 at 17:00!

We will again be meeting at 'Undervisningslokale 1 (U1)' at Dokk1. There's a café at Dokk1 which is open all evening for snacks and beverages.

This meetup will mainly be a social event to catch up on news since last time, but perhaps we are lucky to have a presentation too.

So much is happening on the Swift Evolution forums right now - and Swift 6.3 with Android support was recently released, so we should have plenty of topics to talk about. :-)

Please contact us if you would like to present at the meetup! :-)
