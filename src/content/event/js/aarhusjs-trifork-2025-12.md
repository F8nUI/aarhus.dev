🧑‍🏫 **Talks**\
The topics for this meetup are microfrontends and AI coding agents.

**Microfrontends: Together, But Separately™**\
*By Niclas Skødt Gleesborg, Bankdata*\
Bankdata’s frontend landscape is made up of many independent apps— yet to our customers, it all feels like one experience. This talk explores how we make that possible through collaboration, shared components, and common data. It’s a story about scaling teamwork as much as technology — and how microfrontends become more powerful when they connect.

**Accelerating microfrontends: Transforming legacy with GitHub Copilot**\
*By Erling Grann & Yaseen Khan, Danske Bank*\
Danske Bank’s vast estate of legacy web applications presents significant challenges and exciting opportunities. This talk explores how we are accelerating the development of microfrontends by utilising Github Copilot to generate standardised React microfrontends, enabling faster and more efficient transformation. It’s a story of modernising a complex landscape, driving collaboration, and empowering teams to deliver a unified user experience on a standardised technology stack.
